    Questions couples ask

    Do my guests need to create an account?

    No. Guests open your link, pick a name and photo, and enter your access code once before posting. Browsing needs no code.

    What do I get before I pay?

    A full live preview with your names, date and photos, and a link to share. Nothing is published until you choose a plan.

    How long does my app stay online?

    Every plan includes a first period of hosting, and you can download all photos and videos at any time. The Legacy plan keeps it online for five years.

    Can my photographer and videographer upload their work?

    Yes. Mark them as official vendors and they can upload originals in bulk, credited on every photo.

    Can I use my own domain?

    Your site starts at a name.eventicor.com address. Connecting your own domain is available as an add-on.

    Can I get an app for my phone?

    Yes. When your site is live, one tap installs it on your home screen as an app, on Android or iPhone. You can share the same link or QR code with your guests.

    How do gifts and support work?

    You create a support page on your site. Guests give by card or bank transfer, you see each gift and message, and the money is paid out to your bank account through our payment partner.

    Can I keep using it after the wedding?

    Yes. Add chapters for your honeymoon, anniversary, a baby on the way and more, and choose who can see each one.

    Who can see my photos?

    Only people you invite with your link and access code. You can approve, hide or delete any post, and delete everything whenever you like.
